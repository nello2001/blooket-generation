Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains the data practices of Blooket Generation, a ChatGPT plugin created by Aniello Lombardi.

1. Information processed by the plugin

Blooket Generation is a skills-only ChatGPT plugin. It does not operate its own external application server or user account system.

When you use the plugin, you may choose to provide teaching materials, prompts, documents, or other content in ChatGPT so that question sets can be generated from them.

2. External data collection

Blooket Generation does not independently collect, transmit, sell, or store user data on a server operated by the plugin publisher.

3. ChatGPT and OpenAI

Your use of ChatGPT, including files and messages you submit in ChatGPT, is subject to OpenAI's own terms, privacy policies, retention practices, and product settings. Those practices are controlled by OpenAI, not by Blooket Generation or its publisher.

4. Third-party services

The plugin creates files intended for import into Blooket. Blooket Generation does not automatically upload files to Blooket or access your Blooket account.

If you later upload generated content to Blooket or another service, your use of that service is governed by that service's own terms and privacy practices.

5. Payments

Blooket Generation does not process payments or sell products or services through the plugin.

6. Children and student information

The plugin is intended primarily as a teacher-facing content-generation tool. Users should avoid uploading unnecessary personally identifiable student information and should follow their school, board, employer, and applicable legal requirements when handling student data.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the plugin's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to nellospluginsupport@gmail.com.

Blooket Generation is an independent tool created by Aniello Lombardi. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blooket.

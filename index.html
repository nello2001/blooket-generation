Blooket Generation

Create Blooket-ready multiple-choice question sets from your own uploaded teaching materials.

Designed for teachers who want fast, consistent question generation while staying closely aligned to their lesson notes, worksheets, and unit packages.

What it does

  • Generates multiple-choice questions from materials you provide in ChatGPT.
  • Uses four answer choices with one correct answer by default.
  • Produces a downloadable CSV structured for Blooket spreadsheet import.
  • Matches the terminology, notation, and emphasis found in the supplied materials.
  • Avoids adding outside course content when the source material does not support it.

Typical prompts

  • Create a 20-question Blooket from these lesson notes.
  • Make a 30-question Blooket review using only this uploaded unit.
  • Generate a Blooket CSV from this worksheet.
